01 / THE BRIEF

A clear record of
what happened on site.

Field documentation often arrives as scattered photos, chat messages, and handwritten notes. This project brings the daily record into one calm, searchable workspace.

The interface is built for quick updates on a phone, with dates and weekly grouping that make the archive easy to review later.

↳Design focus
Keep each day easy to capture and each week easy to report.
